# Classic apps: Svelte 5 vs Meltea

Generated by `node bench/classics/run.mjs --frameworks=svelte,meltea`. Each app is written once for
each framework (`<app>/svelte` or `app.mlx`, and `<app>/meltea`), bundled by esbuild, and driven through its
`scenario.mjs` in headless Chromium (Playwright). Per step: median over the runs of the main-thread
busy time (CDP `TaskDuration` delta, from before the step until the page has settled: no DOM mutation
during two animation frames, a task and an idle callback), and of the `LayoutCount` and `RecalcStyleCount`
deltas. Runs per app: 5 at 1x; 5 at 4x except the apps marked with their run count.
Ratio is Meltea / Svelte 5; above 1 Meltea is slower. DOM: the root's and the portal container's
markup after the step (attributes sorted, an empty `class` and empty comments left out, form fields' `value` and `checked` attributes and a `textarea`'s text replaced by their live properties), Svelte 5's first run
against Meltea's first run.

## Summary

- CPU 1x: 36 steps of at least 5 ms under Svelte 5; geometric mean Meltea/Svelte 5 0.88; Meltea slower (> 1.05) in 3, faster (< 0.95) in 24.
- CPU 4x: 36 steps of at least 5 ms under Svelte 5; geometric mean Meltea/Svelte 5 0.89; Meltea slower (> 1.05) in 3, faster (< 0.95) in 23.
